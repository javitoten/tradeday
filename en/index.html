Why a trading journal matters

Most traders don't fail from lack of technical knowledge — they fail from lack of discipline and self-awareness. A journal turns vague feelings into data you can measure and improve. TradeDay logs your trades, reads your behavior and shows you the patterns behind your best and worst decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What is TradeDay?
TradeDay is an online trading journal. You log every trade — and the days you choose not to trade — and the app analyzes your behavior, your metrics and your discipline to help you improve.
Is TradeDay free?
Yes. The Free plan is free forever and includes one trading account with journal, calendar and basic metrics, no credit card required. The Pro plan (9.99€/month) adds unlimited accounts and advanced analysis.
What is a trading journal and why keep one?
A trading journal is a record of all your trades and decisions. It lets you measure your real performance (win rate, risk/reward, best sessions), spot patterns and build the discipline that separates consistent traders from the rest.
Do I need to connect my broker?
No. Logging is manual and private — your data is yours only. You can use TradeDay with any broker, market or instrument (forex, indices, stocks, crypto…).
How is it different from a spreadsheet?
Beyond storing trades, TradeDay analyzes your emotional behavior, calculates your metrics automatically, shows a visual consistency calendar and measures your discipline — with no formulas to maintain.
